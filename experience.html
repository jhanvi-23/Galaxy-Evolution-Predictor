Explore a galaxy. Estimate its properties.

Use the live application to generate stellar-mass and star-formation-rate predictions. The application connects to the separately deployed machine-learning service.

Galaxy Evolution Predictor

The prediction system uses nine GAaP magnitude bands, their associated measurement errors and GaZNet redshift as input features. Two separate trained regression models estimate the two catalogue targets.
